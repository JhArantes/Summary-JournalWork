# 🧠 Full Data Science Cycle — O+ Positivo Newspaper (2022–2024)

## 🎯 Project Overview

As a **Data Scientist** at **O+ Positivo Newspaper**, I was responsible for structuring and operationalizing the entire data lifecycle — from field collection to the strategic delivery of insights to public managers and political stakeholders. This process was essential in supporting the creation of evidence-based public policies, assisting electoral strategies, and strengthening public governance with territorial and population intelligence.

---

## 📥 1. Field Data Collection

### 🔐 Proprietary Software of the Newspaper

- We developed a **custom mobile application** used by field collectors, focusing on **security, usability, and real-time validation**.
- Each researcher was equipped with a **mobile device with GPS**, offline collection, and secure synchronization with central servers.
- The app applied validation rules and standardized formats to ensure **data quality, integrity, and reliability** from the origin.

---

## ☁️ 2. Data Storage and Governance

### 🔸 Cloud Storage (AWS)

- **Amazon S3**: Storage of raw and intermediate data in formats such as JSON and CSV.
- **Amazon RDS**: Use of relational databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL, and Oracle) for structured data and SQL analysis.
- **AWS Lambda**: Execution of automatic functions in response to events (e.g., ingestion of new data).
- **AWS Backup**: Automatic and versioned backups, with periodic monitoring.

### 🔒 Security and Auditing

- **IAM (Identity & Access Management)**: Fine-grained permission and access control based on roles.
- **AWS KMS (Key Management Service)**: Data encryption at rest and in transit.
- **AWS CloudTrail**: Auditing of all actions performed on the data infrastructure.

### 💽 Local Storage (Complementary)

- **Oracle (PL/SQL)**: Relational modeling with procedures, triggers, and packages for data handling and enrichment.
- **MongoDB**: Storage of semi-structured data (e.g., open-ended responses and records with flexible formats).
- **MySQL**: Used in some internal APIs and integrations of legacy applications.

---

## ⚙️ 3. ETL Pipeline (Extract, Transform, Load)

- **AWS Glue**: Automation of ETLs, creation of data catalogs, and orchestration of transformations.
- **Apache Airflow**: Scheduling complex tasks, controlling dependencies, and monitoring workflows.
- **Apache Spark (via AWS EMR)**: Distributed processing of large volumes of data with high performance.

---

## 📊 4. Data Analysis and Modeling

### 🔬 Statistical Analysis and Machine Learning (Python)

- **pandas**: Structuring and manipulating tabular data.
- **numpy**: Matrix calculations and vector operations.
- **scipy**: Advanced statistical analyses, hypothesis testing, and regressions.
- **statsmodels**: Classical modeling — time series, multiple regressions, statistical inference.
- **scikit-learn**: Predictive models (classification, regression, clustering) with reusable pipelines.

### 📉 Data Visualization

- **matplotlib** and **seaborn**: Creation of descriptive and exploratory charts.
- **plotly**: Interactive charts used in web dashboards and presentations.
- **Power BI**: Creation of dynamic and intuitive dashboards aimed at decision-makers, focusing on:
  - Municipal analyses
  - Population segmentation and voting intent
  - Public policy monitoring
  - Regional risks and opportunities

---

## 🚀 5. Value Delivery and Decision-Making

- All processed data was presented in **interactive dashboards in Power BI**, enabling the visualization of critical patterns and trends.
- We held **strategic briefings** with municipal managers, councilors, and candidates, explaining **insights with accessible and action-oriented language**.
- Predictive models helped in **anticipating rejection of public policies**, **resource allocation**, and **electoral campaign planning** based on concrete data.
